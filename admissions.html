Admission Categories

Admissions open for all classes from LKG to 10th Standard for 2026–27

Pre-Primary Admission (LKG & UKG)

Admissions are open for LKG and UKG for the academic year 2026–27. Our Pre-Primary section provides a warm and stimulating learning environment for children beginning their educational journey.

Classes: LKG & UKG
Age Criteria (LKG): 3.5 – 4.5 Years
Age Criteria (UKG): 4.5 – 5.5 Years
Documents: Birth Certificate, Aadhar Card
Enquire Now

Primary & Middle School Admission (1st – 8th Std)

New admissions and transfer admissions are welcome for Classes 1st to 8th Standard. Students joining from other schools will be assessed to ensure appropriate class placement.

Classes: 1st to 8th Standard
Eligibility: Previous class pass certificate
Transfer Cases: TC from previous school required
Documents: TC, Marksheet, Aadhar Card
Enquire Now

Secondary Admission (9th – 10th Std)

Admissions for Classes 9th and 10th Standard are open. Our Secondary Section provides focused academic guidance, experienced subject teachers, and board-exam preparation support for all students.

Classes: 9th & 10th Standard
Eligibility: 8th Std pass for 9th; 9th pass for 10th
Transfer Cases: TC from previous school required
Documents: TC, Marksheet, Aadhar Card, Photos
Enquire Now

Admission Process

Simple and transparent school admission procedure

1

Parent Enquiry

Visit the school or contact us to collect the admission enquiry form and get information about the school.

2

Form Submission

Fill in the admission form with the student's details and submit it along with the required documents.

3

Document Verification

School office will verify submitted documents including birth certificate, previous marksheets, and transfer certificate (if applicable).

4

Admission Confirmation

On successful verification, admission is confirmed. The school will inform about class allocation and uniform details.

5

Fee Payment

Pay the school fee as per the fee structure to complete the admission and receive the school almanac and ID card.
