Art & Craft

Imagination, Creativity & Self-Expression

About the Activity

Art and craft activities encourage imagination, creativity and self-expression. Students can explore different artistic techniques while developing their creative skills.

Activities & Participation

Creative experiences may include:

Drawing

Activities that help children observe, imagine and communicate through lines and shapes.

Painting

Colour-based work that encourages expression and attention to detail.

Craft

Hands-on making that develops patience, coordination and creativity.

Poster Making

Creative design exercises that help students share ideas visually.

Creative Projects

Open-ended projects that encourage imagination and thoughtful making.

Art Competitions

Friendly opportunities for students to present and celebrate creative work.
