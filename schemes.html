Free Admission Under RTE

For Classes: 1st to 8th

Under the Right to Education (RTE) Act, eligible students are provided free admission and education facilities as per government rules and regulations.

Admission Process: As per the rules prescribed by the government.

Raje Yashwantrao Holkar Scheme

Benefit: Free Admission

Under the Raje Yashwantrao Holkar Scheme, eligible students from the Dhangar community are provided free admission and educational facilities as per government rules.

Beneficiaries: Eligible students from the Dhangar community, as per government regulations.
