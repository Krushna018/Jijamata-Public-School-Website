Science Exhibition

Exploration, Experimentation & Creative Thinking

About the Activity

Science exhibitions encourage students to learn through experimentation, observation and creativity. Students get opportunities to present models, experiments and ideas.

Activities & Participation

Students can explore project-based experiences such as:

Science Models

Models that help children explain observations and scientific ideas.

Working Models

Hands-on projects that encourage students to understand how things work.

Experiments

Age-appropriate investigations that build curiosity and careful observation.

Innovation

Creative thinking exercises that support problem-solving and new ideas.

Student Presentations

Opportunities to share learning clearly with classmates and visitors.

Project Displays

Ways for students to present their work and explain their learning.
