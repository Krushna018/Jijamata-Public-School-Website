Our Academic Sections

Four dedicated sections providing quality education from LKG to 10th Standard

Pre-Primary Section (LKG & UKG)

Our Pre-Primary section provides a warm, playful, and stimulating environment for young learners. Through play-based learning, storytelling, and creative activities, we lay the foundations for a child's educational journey.

  • LKG & UKG classes
  • Activity-based and play-based learning
  • Dedicated pre-primary classrooms
  • Trained pre-primary teachers
Enquire Now

Primary Section (1st – 5th Standard)

The Primary Section builds strong literacy, numeracy, and social skills. Subjects are taught through engaging, child-friendly methods with an emphasis on conceptual understanding and joyful learning.

  • Classes 1st to 5th Standard
  • Mathematics, English, Marathi, Hindi, EVS
  • Art, Craft & Physical Education
  • Regular parent–teacher interaction
Enquire Now

Middle School Section (6th – 8th Standard)

The Middle School section broadens students' subject knowledge and fosters independent thinking. Students are introduced to a wider range of subjects and are encouraged to explore their interests through projects and activities.

  • Classes 6th to 8th Standard
  • Mathematics, Science, Social Science
  • English, Marathi, Hindi & Computer Education
  • Science exhibitions and quizzes
Enquire Now

Secondary Section (9th – 10th Standard)

The Secondary Section provides focused, board-exam-oriented education with expert subject teachers, regular revision, and personalised guidance to ensure every student achieves their best results.

  • Classes 9th & 10th Standard
  • Mathematics, Science, Social Science
  • English, Marathi, Hindi & Computer Science
  • Board exam guidance & mock tests
Enquire Now

Science & Mathematics
