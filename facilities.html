School Facilities & Campus Infrastructure

Empowering young minds with modern classrooms, laboratories, sports grounds, & learning amenities

Our Campus Infrastructure

Designed to support academic rigor, physical health, & creative expression for LKG to 10th Standard

Smart Classrooms

Well-ventilated, spacious classrooms equipped with ergonomic seating, smart audio-visual learning boards, and interactive teaching tools for immersive learning.

  • Smart interactive boards
  • Comfortable & safe seating
  • Bright & airy environment

Science Laboratory

Fully equipped science laboratory enabling hands-on practical experiments in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology for middle & secondary school students.

  • Microscopes & specimen collection
  • Physics & Chemistry equipment
  • Strict laboratory safety protocols

Computer Laboratory

Modern computer lab with high-speed internet, educational software, and dedicated computer systems to nurture digital literacy and coding basics.

  • Individual computer workstations
  • Supervised internet access
  • ICT curriculum & basic coding

School Library

A rich collection of academic books, storybooks, encyclopedias, children's literature, magazines, and reference materials encouraging reading habits.

  • 3,000+ curated books
  • Quiet reading room section
  • Weekly reading periods

Playground & Sports Complex

Expansive sports ground supporting Kabaddi, Kho-Kho, Volleyball, Athletics, Cricket, and indoor games like Chess and Carrom.

  • Outdoor sports ground
  • Physical education instructors
  • Annual sports tournaments

Hostel Facilities

Safe and structured residential accommodation with hygienic mess, study hours, and 24/7 warden supervision.
