About JIJAMATA PUBLIC SCHOOL

Established in 2003, Jijamata Public School has been a trusted centre of quality education in Bhenda BK, Ahmednagar, Maharashtra. With over two decades of dedicated service, we have shaped the lives of thousands of students who are now making a positive difference in society.

Our school offers education from LKG to 10th Standard in a caring, disciplined, and stimulating environment. We focus on academic excellence, character building, sports, and co-curricular activities to ensure every child reaches their full potential.
