Student Development Overview

Our commitment to developing every student academically, physically, and creatively

At Jijamata Public School, we believe that education goes beyond textbooks and classrooms. Our Student Development programmes are designed to help every child from LKG to 10th Standard discover their strengths, build confidence, and develop the skills needed to succeed in life.

We organise a rich variety of co-curricular activities, competitions, sports events, cultural programmes, and skill-building workshops throughout the year to ensure students grow holistically alongside their academic studies.

Annual Sports Day & athletic events
Cultural programmes & Annual Day
Science exhibitions & Olympiads
Art, craft & drawing competitions
Elocution & public speaking events
Quiz competitions & educational trips
